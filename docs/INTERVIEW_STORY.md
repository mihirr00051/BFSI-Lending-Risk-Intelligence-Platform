# Interview Story

## 60-second answer
“I built FinSight AI, a synthetic BFSI lending analytics platform. The business problem was that leadership had fragmented customer, loan, repayment and insurance data and could not see portfolio growth and credit risk in one place. I designed a customer-and-loan star-style analytics model, generated BigQuery-ready SQL marts, used Python/R/SAS for profiling and risk analysis, and designed Power BI and Tableau executive dashboards. The project tracks AUM, disbursal, delinquency, NPL proxy, collection efficiency and cross-sell. I also added an AI insight layer that converts KPI patterns into management actions while keeping the outputs grounded in the selected data. The important part was not the dashboard itself; it was defining reliable KPI logic and tracing each insight back to source data.”

## Interview questions to prepare
1. Why did you choose BigQuery instead of querying files directly?
2. How did you define NPL proxy and why is it only a proxy?
3. What data-quality checks would you add before production?
4. How would you prevent KPI disagreement between Power BI and Tableau?
5. How would you handle slowly changing customer attributes?
6. What would you monitor for model/data drift?
7. How would you protect PII and financial data?
8. How would you turn the AI insight layer into a production service?
9. Which metric would you optimize for collections: recovery rate, cost per recovery, or roll rate, and why?
10. What is the difference between portfolio growth and profitable growth?
